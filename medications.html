GERD — Gastro-oesophageal Reflux Disease
💊
Omeprazole / Pantoprazole
Proton Pump Inhibitor (PPI) — First Line
1st LINE
Dose20–40 mg orally once daily
Timing30 minutes before breakfast (essential for efficacy)
Duration4–8 weeks (initial); maintenance if needed
IndicationGERD, peptic ulcer, H. pylori eradication
⭐ Pearl: Must be taken 30 min before a meal — ineffective if taken with food or at bedtime.
💊
Famotidine
H₂ Receptor Antagonist — Second Line
2nd LINE
Dose20–40 mg orally BID or at night
Duration6–8 weeks
IndicationMild GERD, breakthrough nocturnal symptoms
⭐ Pearl: Less effective than PPI. Used if PPI not tolerated or for nocturnal acid breakthrough. Ranitidine withdrawn globally (NDMA contamination).
💊
Metoclopramide
Prokinetic Agent
ADD-ON
Dose10 mg orally TID, 30 min before meals
DurationShort-term only (max 5 days — risk of tardive dyskinesia)
IndicationGastroparesis, GERD with nausea, diabetic gastroparesis
⭐ Pearl: Avoid long-term use — causes extrapyramidal side effects and tardive dyskinesia.

RELIEVER

Nebulizer2.5 mg in 2.5 ml NS every 20 min × 3 (acute attack)

COPD

IndicationCOPD maintenance (GOLD 2–4), reduces exacerbations
⭐ Pearl: First-choice maintenance drug for COPD in most guidelines. Use with caution in urinary retention, angle-closure glaucoma.

⭐ Pearl: NEVER use LABA as monotherapy in asthma — must always be combined with ICS. Contraindicated alone in asthma (increases mortality).

IndicationAsthma Step 2+, COPD with frequent exacerbations (ICS/LABA combo)
⭐ Pearl: Rinse mouth after each use to prevent oral candidiasis. ICS is the cornerstone of asthma management — do not stop suddenly.

⭐ Pearl: COPD exacerbation: 40mg × 5 days — no taper needed for short courses. Longer courses require gradual taper. Add PPI (omeprazole) to protect gastric mucosa.

Cardiology — Hypertension, Heart Failure, PMR

ACE Inhibitor (ACEI)
1st LINE
Lisinopril10 mg PO OD → titrate to 40 mg max
Enalapril5 mg PO BID → titrate to 20 mg BID
IndicationHTN with DM, CKD with proteinuria, Heart failure, Post-MI

IndicationHTN, Heart failure, DM nephropathy (especially with proteinuria), ACEi cough intolerance
⭐ Pearl: Same indications as ACEi but NO cough. Do NOT combine with ACEi (risk of hyperkalaemia and AKI).

ELDERLY / AFK

IndicationHTN (elderly, Afro-Caribbean), Stable angina, Isolated systolic HTN
⭐ Pearl: Preferred in elderly and Afro-Caribbean patients. Side effects: ankle oedema, flushing, headache. Oedema is dose-dependent — not fluid overload.
💊

Thiazide Diuretic
STEP 1–2

Indapamide1.25–2.5 mg PO OD
IndicationHTN (elderly, Afro-Caribbean, isolated systolic), heart failure (NYHA I–II)
⭐ Pearl: Monitor K⁺ (causes hypokalaemia), glucose (can unmask DM), uric acid (avoid in gout).
❤️
Atenolol / Metoprolol / Bisoprolol
Beta-Blocker (β1-Selective)
POST-MI / HF

Bisoprolol2.5–10 mg PO OD (HF: start low, titrate up)
Carvedilol6.25 mg PO BID → titrate to 25 mg BID (HF)
IndicationHTN with post-MI, angina, heart failure, AF rate control
⭐ Pearl: Avoid in COPD/asthma (bronchospasm). Carvedilol = non-selective — covers both β1 + β2 + α1.
💊
Furosemide (Frusemide)
Loop Diuretic
HEART FAILURE
Oral40 mg PO OD (morning); up to 80–160 mg/day
IV acute40–80 mg IV slow (acute pulmonary oedema)
IndicationAcute pulmonary oedema, heart failure with fluid overload, elevated JVP
⭐ Pearl: Give in the morning — avoid nocturnal diuresis. Monitor K⁺ (hypokalaemia), creatinine (pre-renal AKI).
💊
Spironolactone / Eplerenone
Aldosterone Antagonist (MRA)
HF ADD-ON
Spironolactone25–50 mg PO OD (HF: reduce mortality)
Eplerenone25 mg PO OD → titrate to 50 mg OD
IndicationHeart failure (NYHA II–IV, EF ≤35%), post-MI with HF
⭐ Pearl: Risk of hyperkalaemia — avoid if K⁺ >5.0 or eGFR <30. Spironolactone causes gynaecomastia → use eplerenone instead.
💊
Prednisolone — PMR
Systemic Corticosteroid
PMR
Starting dose15–20 mg PO OD (dramatic response = diagnostic)
TaperReduce by 2.5 mg every 2–4 weeks to maintenance ~5 mg OD
Duration12–24 months total
Giant Cell Arteritis60 mg/day — high dose if GCA suspected (to protect vision)

Corticosteroid — Local Injection
CTS
Dose40 mg methylprednisolone + 1 ml lidocaine 1% into carpal tunnel
IndicationMild-moderate CTS (failed conservative management)
Max repeats1–2 injections (risk of tendon rupture with repeated injections)
⭐ Pearl: Inject ulnar to palmaris longus tendon at wrist crease. Provides 3–6 months relief. If symptoms return → surgical decompression.
💊
Colchicine / Allopurinol
Gout Treatment
GOUT
Colchicine (acute)500 mcg PO BID–TID × 2–5 days (acute attack)
Allopurinol (prophylaxis)100 mg PO OD, titrate to 300 mg OD (start 2–4 weeks AFTER acute attack settles)
IndicationAcute gout attack (colchicine/NSAID/steroids), chronic hyperuricaemia (allopurinol)
⭐ Pearl: Never start allopurinol during an acute attack — can prolong or worsen it. Cover with colchicine 500 mcg OD when initiating allopurinol (for first 6 months).

Dexamethasone — Croup
Corticosteroid
CROUP
Dose0.6 mg/kg PO/IM single dose (max 10 mg)
Severe croupAdd nebulized Adrenaline 0.5 ml/kg (1:1000) + nebulized Budesonide 2 mg

Desmopressin (DDAVP) — Nocturnal Enuresis
ADH Analogue
ENURESIS
Oral0.2 mg PO nocte (1 hour before sleep)
Nasal spray20 mcg (2 puffs = 10 mcg each) intranasally at night
IndicationPrimary nocturnal enuresis ≥5 years, failed alarm therapy, pre-event suppression
⭐ Pearl: Also used in Von Willebrand Type 1 and mild haemophilia A. Restrict fluid 1 hour before to 8 hours after — risk of hyponatraemia if fluid overload.
🧒
Hypoglycaemia Management — T1DM
15-15 Rule + Glucagon
HYPOGLYCAEMIA
Conscious15 g fast carbs (3–4 glucose tablets, 150ml juice) → recheck in 15 min → repeat if still low
UnconsciousGlucagon 1 mg IM/SC (child <25 kg: 0.5 mg); or Dextrose 50% 25 ml IV
After recoveryGive complex carbohydrate snack (biscuit + milk) to prevent relapse
⭐ Pearl: The "15-15 rule": 15 g of fast carbs → wait 15 min → if BG <4 mmol/L → repeat. If glucagon given → do not give orally until fully alert (aspiration risk).
Dermatology — Tinea Capitis, Scabies, Pityriasis Rosea
🧴
Terbinafine / Griseofulvin — Tinea Capitis
Systemic Antifungal (Oral — Essential)
ORAL ONLY
Terbinafine (adult)250 mg PO OD × 4 weeks
Terbinafine (child)62.5 mg OD (<20 kg); 125 mg OD (20–40 kg); 250 mg OD (>40 kg) × 4–6 weeks
Griseofulvin20–25 mg/kg/day PO (max 1 g/day) × 6–8 weeks
AdjunctKetoconazole 2% shampoo 2–3×/week (reduces shedding, does NOT cure alone)
⭐ Pearl: Topical antifungals CANNOT penetrate the hair shaft — oral treatment is mandatory. Terbinafine is now preferred over griseofulvin (shorter course, better for dermatophytes).

Permethrin 5% Cream — Scabies
Topical Scabicide
SCABIES

🧴
Cetirizine / Loratadine — Pityriasis Rosea
Non-sedating Antihistamine
SYMPTOMATIC

Loratadine10 mg PO OD (morning, non-sedating)

ADDISON'S

Emergency Medicine
🚨
100% Oxygen — CO Poisoning
Inhaled Oxygen Therapy
FIRST STEP
First step100% O₂ via non-rebreather mask — start immediately
DurationUntil carboxyHb <5% (typically 4–6 hours with 100% O₂)
Hyperbaric O₂2.5–3 ATA × 60–90 min — if: coma, cardiac arrhythmia, carboxyHb >25%, neurological symptoms, or pregnancy
⭐ Pearl: Normal SpO₂ does NOT rule out CO poisoning (pulse oximetry cannot distinguish oxyHb from carboxyHb). Measure co-oximetry (ABG). Pregnant patients and children always get hyperbaric O₂.

Alpha + Beta Adrenergic Agonist
ANAPHYLAXIS
